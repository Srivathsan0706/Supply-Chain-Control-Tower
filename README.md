# 📖 Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Control Tower** is an end-to-end supply chain analytics project designed to provide centralized visibility into critical operational functions. The project integrates data preparation, SQL-based business analysis, KPI validation, DAX measure development, and interactive Power BI dashboards to provide centralized visibility across inventory, transportation, supplier performance, demand forecasting, warehouse operations, and order fulfillment.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow—from raw operational datasets and SQL analysis to KPI validation and executive dashboards—enabling data-driven decision-making through meaningful business insights.

# 🎯 Business Problem

Supply chain operations generate large volumes of operational data across multiple business functions. Without centralized reporting, organizations often face challenges such as:

- Limited visibility into inventory performance
- Delayed identification of supply chain risks
- Inefficient transportation monitoring
- Difficulty evaluating supplier performance
- Inaccurate demand forecasting
- Limited visibility into order fulfillment performance

The objective of this project is to consolidate operational data into a centralized Supply Chain Control Tower that enables faster, data-driven business decisions.

- Build a centralized Supply Chain Control Tower dashboard.

- Validate Power BI KPIs with SQL queries.

# 📂 Dataset Overview

The project uses a simulated supply chain dataset consisting of **8 interconnected business tables** representing core operational functions. The datasets were cleaned and transformed in Microsoft Excel, analyzed using SQL, and modeled in Power BI to create an end-to-end analytical solution.

| Dataset | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Orders | Customer orders, demand quantity, delivered quantity, order status, warehouse allocation, and regions. |
| Inventory | Current stock, safety stock, reorder point, lead time, inventory value, and inventory health status. |
| Transportation | Shipment details including carrier, freight cost, delivery days, origin, destination, and routes. |
| Forecast | Forecast quantity, actual demand, warehouse, and monthly demand planning data. |
| Product | Product master containing SKU, product name, category, and unit cost. |
| Supplier | Supplier master with supplier information, defect rate, and on-time delivery (OTD) performance. |
| Supplier Product Map | Mapping between suppliers and products supplied. |
| Warehouse | Warehouse master containing warehouse location and regional information. |

**Total Records Analysed:** ~50,000+

# 📊 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI solution consists of **7 interactive dashboards**, each designed to provide focused visibility into a critical supply chain function.

| Dashboard | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Executive Dashboard | High-level overview of supply chain performance and key KPIs. |
| Inventory Dashboard | Monitor inventory health, stock availability, safety stock, reorder points, and inventory value. |
| Transportation Dashboard | Analyze freight cost, delivery performance, routes, carriers, and shipment efficiency. |
| Supplier Dashboard | Evaluate supplier performance using On-Time Delivery (OTD), defect rates, and supply risk metrics. |
| Forecast Dashboard | Compare forecast demand against actual demand and evaluate forecast accuracy and forecast error. |
| Order Fulfillment Dashboard | Track order status, fill rate, delayed orders, partial deliveries, and regional performance. |
| Warehouse Dashboard | Analyze warehouse inventory, stock distribution, warehouse utilization, and replenishment indicators. |

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The project generated several operational insights across different supply chain functions.

### Inventory

- Identified warehouses with the highest inventory risk.
- Highlighted SKUs operating below safety stock levels.
- Compared inventory value distribution across warehouses and product categories.

### Transportation

- Identified carriers contributing the highest freight spend.
- Compared shipment volumes and delivery performance across carriers.
- Highlighted transportation cost optimization opportunities.

### Supplier Performance

- Identified high-risk suppliers using On-Time Delivery (OTD), Lead Time, and Defect Rate.
- Compared supplier performance against customer demand.
- Highlighted suppliers requiring performance improvement.

### Forecasting

- Measured forecast accuracy and forecast error across warehouses.
- Compared forecast demand against actual demand.
- Identified warehouses with significant demand variance.

### Order Fulfillment

- Evaluated fill rate across warehouses and product categories.
- Identified delayed order trends.
- Compared delivered quantity against customer demand.

### Warehouse Operations

- Compared inventory distribution across warehouses.
- Identified warehouses with higher inventory value.
- Evaluated warehouse inventory health and replenishment indicators.
